## Description

Round-4 whole-surface audit (documents/hermes-v0.21.1-whole-surface-audit-round4.md, C10 section). Needs round-4 product decisions 15 (hoist `Process.waitDraining` into ScarfCore now) and 16 (`enforceArchiveBounds` fail-open vs refuse).

- HIGH · PRE · `unzip`/`zip` in ScarfCore hold unbounded `waitUntilExit()` with undrained stderr and a `readToEnd` after the wait: `RemoteRestoreService.swift:544` (also `:354`), `RemoteBackupService.swift:520`. A chatty/corrupt archive hangs restore or backup forever, no timeout (C10). Fix: hoist `Process.waitDraining` (`scarf/scarf/Core/Models/ProcessTimeout.swift`) into ScarfCore, convert all three with named timeouts.
- HIGH · PRE · `HermesProxyService.swift:86` gives the child an undrained, unclosed stdout `Pipe()`; 64 KB of output wedges the proxy. Fix: `FileHandle.nullDevice`.
- MED · PRE · `enforceArchiveBounds` fails OPEN: `runToolCapturingOutput` (`ProjectTemplateService.swift:342-344,366-391`) reads stdout after the bounded poll and never drains stderr; a chatty `unzip -Zt` times out and `guard let listing = try? … else { return }` skips the entry-count and unpacked-size caps. Decision 16.
- MED · PRE · `HermesFileService.runShellProbe` (`:2620-2633`) reads stdout after the wait, never drains `errPipe`; noisy rc files (>64 KB) block and the env probe returns nil. Use `waitDraining(timeout:pipes:[pipe, errPipe])`.
- MED · PRE · `SpotifyAuthFlow.swift:94-107` never nils its `readabilityHandler`s on EOF (teardown only at `:123-124`) and the spawn has no timeout (C10).
- LOW · PRE · `AppRelauncher.swift:91-95` never closes the WRITE ends of its pipes (2 fds per relaunch). Related: t-b15ba4c3 (main-actor wait).
- LOW · PRE · `MainActorSpawnDisciplineP22Tests.swift:466` matcher misses `isRunning`-polls and `DispatchSemaphore.wait(`/`DispatchGroup.wait(` — check whether P38 item 23 added them; else add.

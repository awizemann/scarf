## Plan

Decision (Alan, 2026-09-09): DELETE the hallucination-gate surface rather than re-derive it. Rationale: Hermes never emits `hallucination_gate_status`/`auto_blocked_reason` on any version; it only appends a `completion_blocked_hallucination` task_event (kanban_db.py:2629 at v2026.9.7) and the stall already surfaces through `last_failure_error` (added Phase 6). If richer diagnosis is wanted later, design it from the real event payload as its own feature. Goal/diagnostics: drop decode paths unless `kanban diagnostics --json` is a one-call off-main addition; decide from source.

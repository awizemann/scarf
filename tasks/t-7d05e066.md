## Plan

Landed (P2, from documents/reports/2026-09-04-projects-post-remediation-audit.md):

1. PERF H1 + DI M6 — `SSHTransport.statAll` reply lines are self-identifying (`\u{1F}s<i> ` printed per path); unmarked lines ignored, duplicate/out-of-range index or a short reply returns nil, newline/CR-containing paths refused before the round-trip (logged). Parsing split into `parseStatAllReply` for testability.
2. PERF M4 — `statAll` returns `[String: FileStat]?`; nil = "don't trust". `ProjectCockpitViewModel.shouldRead(reason:recheckHealth:fresh:last:)` is the decision: a watcher tick with no signature keeps state and retries next tick instead of taking the full derive-and-save path over the sick transport.
3. DI H6 — `WatchBaselineStore.merge(_:)`; the poller's blob fallback merges instead of replacing, so per-path baselines survive a misaligned reply and deltas across the gap are still reported.
4. DI H7 — the empty-reply `continue` (which skipped the sleep) is gone; every loop iteration sleeps.
5. PERF H2 — `projects`, `registryDamage` and `selectedHasDashboard` are equality-guarded; the last behind a single private setter.
6. PERF H3 — the dashboard-glyph stat is gated on selection / registry-change / explicit refresh, with a 60s revalidation floor as the invalidation edge.
7. PERF M3 + DI M7 — `QuarantineMemo` (process-wide, keyed by path + SHA-256 of the bytes, 300s TTL) skips the dedup scan; `.corrupt-*` copies pruned to the newest 5 on write, with memo entries forgotten for what pruning deletes.
8. PERF M1 — `KanbanSummaryWidgetView` resolves the tenant off-main and caches the NEGATIVE result against the manifest's `mtime:size`.
9. PERF M2 — the three sheet-completion `viewModel.load()` sites are `Task { await viewModel.reload() }`; the unmirror runs detached.
10. Cheap wins: fileExists+readFile collapsed at ProjectUpgradeService / MiniAppService / MiniAppStore (ProjectConfigService SKIPPED — it throws, and collapsing would lose absent-vs-unreadable); iOS dashboard poll now compares `mtime:size` and any difference (not strictly-greater).

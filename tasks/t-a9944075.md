## Description

47 `.buttonStyle(.borderedProminent)` call sites (Mac Features/* plus iOS CuratorView) draw the system's white label on the app AccentColor. In dark mode that's about 2.4:1 on #E89360. ScarfPrimaryButton uses onAccent (#3B1608 in dark) but ignores the disabled state and controlSize. Plan: (1) teach ScarfPrimaryButton to handle isEnabled (dimmed fill and label), controlSize (small/regular/large padding and type), and pressed/hover states; (2) migrate every call site, keeping .keyboardShortcut(.defaultAction) and .controlSize; (3) fix the iOS CronListView:105 swipe tint, which draws a white "Duplicate" label on the accent; (4) add a lint in tools/check-design-tokens.py that fails on `.borderedProminent` in app and package sources; (5) build both apps and take light/dark screenshots of a few migrated screens.

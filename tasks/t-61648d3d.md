## Description

Step 1 of the free voice path (decision 2026-09-19, report documents/plans/2026-09-19-voice-p7-free-voice-path.md design A). ScarfCore only. Protocols first (P7b/P7c bind to them):
- `VoiceListener` (start → AsyncStream of .partial(text)/.final(text)/.level(Double)/.failed(error); stop; pause/resume for barge-in) with `AppleOnDeviceVoiceListener` (SFSpeechAudioBufferRecognitionRequest, requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true, AVAudioEngine tap, end-of-utterance on silence per voice.silence_duration-style timeout; AVAudioSession only under os(iOS)).
- `VoiceSpeaker` (speak(text) async, stop(), isSpeaking) with `HermesVoiceSpeaker` (HermesSpeechService + HermesTTSCache, magic-byte audio, AVAudioPlayer) and `SystemVoiceSpeaker` (AVSpeechSynthesizer); `FallbackVoiceSpeaker` chains Hermes → system.
- `ChainedVoiceEngine: VoiceConversationEngine` half-duplex loop: listening → final transcript → stop phrase check (VoiceLiveText.isVoiceStopCommand) → submitVoiceTurn (plain text, origin voice, no OpenAI turn note) → stream reply → speak sentence chunks (VoiceLiveText chunking/sanitize) → listen again. Captions, micLevel, elapsed, cost 0, notices, idle auto-end reuse. Barge-in: keep listening during playback and stop the speaker on speech onset (grace window).
- `VoiceLiveAvailability` gains `.chainedReady` (hasHermesSpeechSynthesis, 0.20.1, and mode chained) alongside `.ready` (gpt-live); `.hidden(.chainedMode)` goes away; `VoiceEngineKind` tells the apps which engine to mount.
- `VoiceDataRecipient.forMode(.chained)` stays nil (no consent; audio never leaves the device).
Tests with fake listener/speaker/turn host mirroring GPTLiveEngineTests; availability tests updated.

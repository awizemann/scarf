## Description

Found in P2 (t-e267092a). HermesTTSCache's voice fingerprint doesn't include global `tts.speed` or `tts.providers.<name>.*` (command/plugin providers), so after changing them, previously spoken messages replay old audio. Fix: fingerprint a stable hash of the whole parsed `tts` section (simpler than per-key modelling). Pick up in the whole-surface audit round.

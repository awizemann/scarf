P48 gave these spawns `onTermination` + stderr drain but left the read loop. Decision 10: port ACP's `DispatchSourceRead` line reader, or move the loop to `Thread.detachNewThread`.
- MED · PRE · `CitadelServerTransport.runSync` (`ScarfIOS/CitadelServerTransport.swift:683-699`) blocks a pool thread on a pool task; P53 bounded it with `runSync(deadline:)` but the seam is still sync; iOS call sites wrap it in `Task.detached` (`MemoryListView.swift:98-106`, `ChatView.swift:1459`, `ProfilesView.swift:190`, `WebhooksView.swift:105`, eight in `ScarfCore/ViewModels`). Decision 11: make the seam async (`asyncRunProcess` exists) — the t-02f830f4 work, iOS half first.
- MED · PRE · Five main-actor spawn/env sites (t-f30054a8): `SpotifyAuthFlow.swift:133` (literal twin of the `NousAuthFlow.start()` fix in c93c2287), `OAuthFlowController.swift:252` and `MCPLoginController.swift:237` (inside `defaultProcess(_:)` from `start()`; remote path reaches `enrichedEnvironment()` via `SSHTransport.makeProcess` → `sshSubprocessEnvironment()`), `HealthViewModel.swift:1182`, `HermesProxyService.swift:82` + `proc.run()` at `:130` (`start()`; `stop()` was fixed). `enrichedShellEnv` is a `swift_once` of two zsh probes (5 s + 3 s, `HermesFileService.swift:2566-2583`).
- LOW · PRE · `ProcessACPChannel.swift:242-246` close watchdog terminates but never escalates to SIGKILL.
- LOW · PRE · `HermesLogService.swift:82-84` claims iOS streams via a Citadel exec channel; `CitadelServerTransport.streamLines:158-168` is an M3 stub. Doc fix or wire it.
- ~26 remaining blocking `Task.detached` sites: t-406d56d6.

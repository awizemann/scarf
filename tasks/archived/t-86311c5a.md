## Description

Round-5 whole-surface audit (documents/hermes-v0.21.1-whole-surface-audit-round5.md, C10 section). Needs round-5 product decisions 4–8. Folds t-10eb7c17 items and t-12d04477; relates t-f3820038, t-f3f2602c, t-6ba8c1ce, t-f43f0af5.

- HIGH · PRE · `ProcessPipeDrainer.Capture.wait()` (`ProcessPipeDrainer.swift:43-46`) is an unbounded `group.wait()`; both transports' timeout arms call `terminate()` → bare `waitUntilExit()` → `wait()` (`LocalTransport.swift:322-330`, `SSHTransport.swift:1116-1131`), so an inherited write end (ssh ControlMaster, a hermes worker) hangs the "timeout" path forever. Contrast `ProcessPipeDrain.collect(grace:)`.
- HIGH · PRE · `ProcessPipeDrainer` reads on `DispatchQueue.global(.utility)` (`:51,:57`) — the anti-pattern P43c fixed in `ProcessPipeDrain` (`ProcessTimeout.swift:243-255`); plausible cause of the `M0bTransportTests` flake. Decision 4: retire onto `startDraining`/`waitDraining` or patch in place.
- HIGH · PRE · Four streaming spawns (`LocalTransport.swift:365-408,:422-471`; `SSHTransport.swift:732-805,:814-877`; caller `HermesLogService.swift:87`) have no `continuation.onTermination`, never terminate the child, and block a cooperative thread in `availableData`. Decision 6.
- MED · PRE · `TestConnectionProbe.swift:182-202` reads both pipes after the wait (t-10eb7c17 item 4) — verbose ssh >64 KB manufactures "Timed out after 20s".
- MED · PRE · `SSHScriptRunner.swift:267-277` (and `runLocally` ~`:329-350`) nils readabilityHandlers on `!isRunning` and snapshots before EOF — the OAuthFlowController class; cure is `ProcessOutputInbox`.
- MED · PRE · `HermesProxyService.stop()` (`:145-149`) SIGTERM with no escalation/ceiling.
- MED · PRE · `LocalTransport.runProcess`/`SSHTransport.runLocal` are synchronous (100 ms spin + unbounded `group.wait()`) called from async code; the P43c sweep cannot see them. Decision 5: make `timeout` non-optional; move onto the async primitive.
- MED · PRE · `AnalyticsFeatureUsageEventsTests` `.serialized` against a process-global tracker (`Analytics.swift:189`); `AppCoordinator.init` emits `section_viewed` from `CronViewAccessibilityTreeTests.swift:92`, `SidebarRestructureTests.swift:119,:137`. Inject the tracker or assert on a nonce — prerequisite for any test parallelism.
- MED · PRE · `M4ACPIOSTests:118` cancels on a 50 ms nap (`waitFor` default 2 s at `:226`); `ACPClientStartIdempotenceTests:108` passes vacuously on a 50 ms nap and has unbounded gates `:70-71,:238-239`; `M0bTransportTests:157-180` spends its 10 s on 512 shell forks (use `head -c … /dev/zero`).
- LOW · PRE · Transports' launch-failure arm leaks 2 fds (`LocalTransport.swift:292-300`, `SSHTransport.swift:1078-1088`); `HermesProxyService.swift:137-140` same; `runProcess` `timeout == nil` arm bare `waitUntilExit()`; `MainActorSpawnDisciplineP22Tests.swift:251` 900 ms nap; guard-style inconsistency `#if os(macOS)` vs `#if !os(iOS)` (`SSHScriptRunner.swift:122,:362`).
- Fixtures (decision 8): `ProjectTemplateBoundsP43Tests:123` 300 MB bomb, `ProcessDrainP43Tests:96,:176,:376-420` 24/64 MB — ~30 s of the 121 s serial run; start the clock before `Process.run()` and shrink.
- Remaining stability-sweep sites repo-wide: 90 in 35 files (t-f43f0af5) — decision 7.

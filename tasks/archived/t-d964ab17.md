## Artifacts

Closed by P48 (`t-86311c5a`), commit `86b49af1` on `fix/whole-surface-audit-r5`. It was the fixture, exactly as decision 8 suspected — not a drain defect.

`M0bTransportTests.localTransportRunProcessDrainsLargeStdoutAndStderr` produced its 256 KB per stream with two `for i in $(seq 1 256)` loops that each called `seq 1 1018` again: **512 shell forks**. Under parallel load those forks, not the drain, consumed the 10 s ceiling, and the failure was reported as `.timeout(partialStdout: 102405 bytes)` for a transport that was working perfectly.

`head -c 262144 /dev/zero | tr '\000' 'x'` is two processes per stream and produces the same volume in milliseconds. The test now runs in **0.06 s** against its unchanged 10 s ceiling — a 160x margin — and asserts an exact byte count rather than `>=`.

Verified green in isolation and in two full `swift test` runs (3038 tests / 233 suites, no reruns).

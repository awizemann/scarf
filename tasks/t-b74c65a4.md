## Description

Found in P56 (round-6 whole-surface audit, `t-19ba24a5`) while enumerating every validation `hermes cron edit` performs against the iOS form (addendum lesson 14).

`CronEditorView` (`scarf/Scarf iOS/Cron/CronListView.swift:360-385`) offers a Kind picker with `cron` / `interval` / `once`, but has **no field for `schedule.minutes`** — the only thing an `interval` schedule is made of. `buildJob` (`:530`) carries the stored `minutes` forward only while the kind is unchanged, so a NEW interval job, or one switched to `interval` from `cron`/`once`, writes `{"kind": "interval", "minutes": null}` into `cron/jobs.json`. `compute_next_run` returns `None` for an interval without `minutes` (`cron/jobs.py:1106-1110` @ `v2026.9.7`) and `_recover_missing_next_run` (`:2690-2710`) cannot arm it either: the job says "scheduled" in the list forever and never fires.

P56 made that a REFUSAL rather than a silent dead job — `HermesCronJob.scheduleFormRefusal` answers `.intervalMinutesMissing` and `isValid` greys Save with a message naming the Mac app — because refusing is the validation, and growing the form a field is a feature. This task is the feature.

Scope:
- A minutes `TextField` (numeric) in the Schedule section, shown when `scheduleKind == "interval"`, seeded from `initial?.schedule.minutes`.
- `buildJob` writes it into `CronSchedule.minutes`.
- Drop `.intervalMinutesMissing` from the refusal set, or repoint it at "enter a minutes value" once the field exists — `CronScheduleFormRefusalP56Tests.anIntervalWithNoCarriedMinutesIsRefused` and `theIntervalGateAsksWhatTheSaveWouldWrite` pin the current behaviour and must be updated with it.
- Hermes stores `minutes` as an int and `_interval_schedule` builds `{"kind": "interval", "minutes": n, "display": "every {n}m"}` (`cron/jobs.py:729-730`), so a `display` that still says the old cadence is the same staleness `scheduleMoved` already handles.
- Mac has no twin to change: `CronView` posts a free-text `--schedule` to `parse_schedule`, which builds `minutes` itself.

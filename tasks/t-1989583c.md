## Description

Found 2026-10-05 in the button-contrast slice. ScarfBadge(.danger) text on its 16% tint is 4.23 light / 4.19 dark on the card. The other kinds (success, warning, info) are likely similar and aren't measured. site/styles.css uses --red #d9534f as text in its widget status pills (3.8:1). In design/static-site/ui-kit/Common.jsx, the outline danger button and the danger menu item use red-600 text (about 3.0:1 on the dark card), and their red-100 hover would fail once switched to var(--danger). Fix: measure every semantic text-on-tint pair (badges, pills, status tiles) in both modes, adjust tint alpha or text step, mirror to the CSS, and extend tools/check-design-tokens.py to cover these pairs.

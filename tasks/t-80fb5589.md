## Deliberate deviations from the ticket

1. **`hasSharedMetricsSend`, not `hasSharedMetricsTelemetry`** — that name is already a v0.20 flag for the sibling `telemetry.shared_metrics.enabled` collection switch (unchanged at v0.21.1); only `.send`/`.endpoint` are new. The v0.20 flag's "no remote sink" doc was corrected to point at the new one.
2. **`hasKeenableWebBackend` floors at v0.20.5, not v0.20.6** — `git ls-tree <tag> plugins/web/` across every tag shows `keenable` first at v2026.8.19. The audit report's "since v0.20.6" is wrong.
3. **`hasTavilyWebBackend` is a window (`semver != 0.21.0`), not a floor** — tavily is present ≤0.20.6, gone at 0.21.0, back at 0.21.1.
4. **Roster extracted to ScarfCore** rather than tested in place: `WebToolsTab` lives in the app target where no test bundle can reach it, so finding D's parity test needed a testable seam.
5. **Lane 4 resolves plugin ids through ALIASES** before reporting — without it `ai-gateway`/`kilocode`/`opencode-zen` are false alarms (they're aliases of vercel/kilo/opencode).

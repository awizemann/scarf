## Description

P40 ported `MCPLoginController`'s drain choreography into `OAuthFlowController` (commit `ef79ccd4`) and hoisted only its buffer — `OutputInbox` → `ScarfCore.ProcessOutputInbox` (`scarf/Packages/ScarfCore/Sources/ScarfCore/Services/ProcessOutputInbox.swift`). The rest (generation token, `pendingExit`/`didFinish`, `pump()`, `scheduleDrainDeadline`, the off-main spawn with its generation-mismatch unhook, the `ProcessFactory` seam) is now duplicated verbatim in two files:

- `scarf/scarf/Features/MCPServers/ViewModels/MCPLoginController.swift:96-218`
- `scarf/scarf/Features/CredentialPools/ViewModels/OAuthFlowController.swift:130-300`

Three more sites carry a thinner version of the same shape and would benefit:
- `scarf/scarf/Core/Services/NousAuthFlow.swift:118`
- `scarf/scarf/Core/Services/SpotifyAuthFlow.swift:109` (P43 owns its readabilityHandler/timeout finding)
- `scarf/scarf/Core/Services/HermesProxyService.swift:102` (P43 owns its stdout pipe finding)

P40 deliberately did NOT do the hoist: P43 owns two of the five, and the differences (stdin writes and a live "Authorization code:" prompt in `OAuthFlowController`, a remote `pkill` reap in `MCPLoginController`, no verdict at all in `HermesProxyService`) meant a shared type would have been a rewrite with a bigger blast radius than the bug being fixed. Do it after P43 lands, when all five call sites are in their final shape.

A `DrainedProcessRun` should own: the pipe + decoder + inbox, the generation token, the EOF-and-exit join, the drain deadline, the off-main spawn, and the retire path — and hand the owner a `(exitCode, output)` once, plus a per-chunk callback for live parsing.

## Plan

**P43 landed (2026-09-11); re-checked, still NOT a genuine reduction — left filed.**

P43 owned two of the five sites and both are now in their final shape:
- `SpotifyAuthFlow` — the two `readabilityHandler`s fold into one shared `@Sendable [weak self]` closure that unhooks on EOF, and the spawn has a 240 s deadline task. It has no exit-code verdict, no inbox, no generation token and no drain deadline; its "verdict" is a poll of `auth.json` AFTER the child exits.
- `HermesProxyService` — stdout is `FileHandle.nullDevice` now and it still has no verdict at all: it is a long-lived server whose termination handler flips `isRunning`, not a one-shot run that produces `(exitCode, output)`.

So the two P43 touched moved FURTHER from the proposed `DrainedProcessRun` contract ("hand the owner a `(exitCode, output)` once"), not closer. The genuine duplication is still exactly the two P40 named — `MCPLoginController:96-218` and `OAuthFlowController:130-300` — and the differences P40 listed (live stdin + an "Authorization code:" prompt in one, a remote `pkill` reap in the other) are unchanged. What P43 did hoist was the level below: `Process.waitDraining` now lives in `ScarfCore/Models/ProcessTimeout.swift`, so a future `DrainedProcessRun` can be built on it from either target.

Scope it to the TWO controllers, not five.
